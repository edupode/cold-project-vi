## Julia's Launcher {tag} is out 🎞️
**New**
🤝 The **VIctory-NVIDIA** fork is merged in (thanks trustno1-coder and its contributors): DLSS Frame Generation and AMD FSR 3 frame generation, the game's 30 fps limit lifted, open water without dark blocks, PS4/PS5 pads, Windows 10 support.
🎞️ More **frame generation** choices on Play: Automatic, NVIDIA Smooth Motion, DLSS Frame Generation (RTX 40/50, RTX 20/30 with MFG Unlocker), FSR 3 (RTX) or VIctory 2X.
⚙️ A new **F1 menu**: sections, what each setting does, save sharing; DLSS and the frame generation multiplier change there.
🎨 Launcher **themes**: Plum, Midnight, Vice, Graphite, Daylight.

**Fixes**
🔊 TVs, phone calls and reverb are heard.
🧱 Fewer crashes from uploads, and a new victory.exe warms up and restarts once by itself.

⏳ Shaders may get prepared once more after this update.

**Install**
🔄 On 0.3.x: nothing to do, it installs itself within 5 minutes while the game is closed.
📥 New here or on an older launcher: download **VI-Setup-{version}.exe** from https://github.com/felicienradio/cold-project-vi/releases/latest and run it.

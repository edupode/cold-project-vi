Julia's Launcher 0.3.17: the VIctory-NVIDIA fork merged in, more frame generation choices, and a new F1 menu.

- The VIctory-NVIDIA fork (trustno1-coder and its contributors) is merged in: DLSS Frame Generation and AMD FSR 3 frame generation through NVIDIA Streamline, the game's own 30 fps limit lifted, open water without the dark blocks, PS4 and PS5 pads, and the Windows 10 Direct3D 12 runtime.
- Frame generation on the Play page: Automatic, NVIDIA Smooth Motion, DLSS Frame Generation (RTX 40/50; RTX 20/30 with MFG Unlocker, someone else's mod, fetched only if you pick it), FSR 3 (RTX cards, with DLSS upscaling) or VIctory 2X. NVIDIA's and AMD's files are fetched from their own GitHub at PLAY.
- F1 in the game: a redesigned settings menu (sections, what each setting does), save sharing, and restarts that keep their options. DLSS and the frame generation multiplier can be changed there even when the launcher started the game.
- Launcher themes: Plum, Midnight, Vice, Graphite and Daylight, and a cleaner Play page.
- The first start of a new victory.exe warms up and starts again once, by itself.
- Sound: TVs, phone calls and reverb are heard.
- Fewer crashes: room for uploads is reserved at the start, and upload buffers are reused instead of one new buffer per copy.
- Shaders may be prepared once more: the shader translator changed.

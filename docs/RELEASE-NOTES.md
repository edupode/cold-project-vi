Julia's Launcher 0.3.27: more FPS on the weak presets, a "Beefy PC mode", seven languages, and the most common crash of 0.3.25 fixed.

- More FPS on Ultra Low and Modest PC: the game's own settings are made lighter before it starts (shadows at 1280 px; on Ultra Low also a nearer detail distance and fewer small objects far away), from VIctory-Hydra. Any other preset can have the same with the new "Lighter game detail" option; Advanced Tweaks has a control for each.
- "Recommended" is now "Beefy PC mode", and it keeps the game's full detail.
- With DLSS at the output's own size (DLAA) the game ran out of its render-target memory and closed a second after starting: the most common crash of 0.3.25 (52 reports, 12 players). DLAA now runs as DLSS quality.
- The launcher checks the game's folder against the complete game and says what is missing before you play: every game from an incomplete folder closed within seconds.
- Languages: a picker with English, Português, Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano and Русский; on a first start, Windows' own language. New languages are files anyone can add (CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Game logs are read by edupode and the project's team; players who said yes before are asked once more, and nothing is sent until they answer.
- 16 GB PCs can keep less idle memory (VICTORY_STAGING_KEPT, VICTORY_MAX_PRELUDES).
